Hot Enough: Thermometers

Run the lab procedure here, then answer the questions on your worksheet. You will watch how the volume of a liquid and the volume of a gas change with temperature.

Mark the liquid level in five conditions

  1. Choose a condition to put the ethylene glycol thermometer in.
  2. Watch the liquid in the straw. Wait until it stops moving.
  3. Mark the level with the marker. Repeat for all five conditions.

Trap a sample of air and change its temperature

  1. The beaker holds 200 mL of water with two drops of food coloring, heated to about 80 °C. Turn the dry 10 mL graduated cylinder upside down and lower it until its mouth almost touches the bottom.
  2. Hold it in the hot water for at least one minute. Watch the mouth of the cylinder.
  3. Quickly move the cylinder to the beaker of room-temperature water. Keep the mouth near the bottom.
  4. Leave the cylinder where it is and add ice to the water.
